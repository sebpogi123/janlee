NCR Regional Meet · Wushu
Janlee Diaz represented Pasig in wushu, a combat sport, at the NCR Regional Meet last school year.Did you know? Wushu is a Chinese martial art with two main events: taolu (forms) and sanda (full-contact sparring).

  • Horse stance hold3 holds of 30–45 sec. Builds leg strength and a solid base.Easy start: Hold for 15–20 seconds and stand a little higher.
  • Stance transitions3 sets of 10. Move smoothly between stances for balance and control.
  • Front and side kicks3 sets of 10 per leg. Practice slowly for form, then add speed.
  • Jump rope and jumps5 rounds of 1 min. Explosive legs for jumping techniques.Easy start: Do 2–3 rounds of 30 seconds, or just hop in place.
  • Hip and leg stretching10 min after training. Flexibility for high kicks and splits.

Fun Week Singles Champion
Raiana Jade Pedraza won the badminton singles category at the APEC Schools intramurals, called Fun Week.Did you know? In singles, a player covers the whole court alone, so footwork and stamina matter as much as smashes.

  • Split-step drill3 sets of 20 hops. Faster first step toward the shuttle.Easy start: Do 2 sets of 10 small hops, no rush.
  • Lunges to the corners3 sets of 10 per side. Court movement and leg strength.
  • Shuttle runs6 x 10 meters. Agility and recovery between rallies.Easy start: Jog 3 x 10 meters at an easy pace.
  • Wrist and forearm curls3 sets of 15 with a light weight. Better racket control.
  • Shoulder circles2 sets of 15 each way. Warm-up that helps protect smashes.
  • Squat jumps3 sets of 10. Higher spikes and blocks.Easy start: Do plain squats first, 3 sets of 8, then add a small jump.
  • Wall passing3 sets of 20 against a wall. Cleaner forearm passes.
  • Push-ups3 sets of 10–15. Arm and chest strength for serves.Easy start: Do knee push-ups, 3 sets of 5–8.
  • Lateral shuffles4 x 10 meters. Quicker defense and court cover.Easy start: Walk the side steps slowly over 5 meters.
  • Calf raises3 sets of 15. Stronger, more stable landings.
  • Ball-handling drills10 min dribbling with both hands. Better control under pressure.
  • Defensive slides4 x 10 meters. Quick lateral movement.Easy start: Slide slowly for 2 x 5 meters with a low, steady stance.
  • Box or step jumps3 sets of 8. Explosive power for rebounds.Easy start: Step up and down on a low step, 3 sets of 6.
  • Free-throw practice50 shots, tracked. Consistency late in the game.
  • Interval sprints6 x 20 seconds, 40 sec rest. Match-pace conditioning.Easy start: Jog 4 x 15 seconds with 60 seconds of rest.

Warm up for 5–10 minutes first, drink water, and stop if something hurts. Ask your coach or PE teacher to adjust sets and reps to your level.

Study Balance

Plan your week

Write down practice, games and deadlines every Sunday. Put study blocks around them, not instead of them.

Study in short blocks

Try 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break. Do the hardest subject when you have the most energy.

Use travel and rest time

Review notes or flashcards on the way to games and between matches.

Sleep 8 hours

Sleep helps both memory and muscle recovery. Avoid all-night cramming before a game day.

Talk to your teachers

Tell them your game schedule early so you can agree on make-up work or extra time.

Combat sports

Training is intense, so study right after a meal and a rest, not when you are exhausted.

Badminton

Tournament days run long. Bring notes for the waiting time between matches.

Volleyball and basketball

Team trips and evening games are common, so finish tough homework before game days.

KaKaMPI

Our MCH (Mindset, Character, Habits) subject is about becoming better each day, in the classroom and on the court. Here is how it applies to athletes.

Mindset

See losses and hard tests as practice. Set small goals for each training and each lesson.

Character

Play fair, respect teammates and opponents, and own your mistakes.

Habits

Warm up, sleep, study and review at the same times every day until they stick.

  • KaKahusayanExcellence: give your best effort in every drill and every subject.
  • KaKatapatanLoyalty: stay committed to your team, your school and your goals.
  • MMalasakitCompassion: help teammates who are struggling in sports or studies.
  • PPagkamalikhainCreativity: find new plays, new routines and new ways to study.
  • IIntegridadIntegrity: do the right thing, even when no one is watching.
